What is DAYSTAR, and who is it for?

DAYSTAR is a personal-growth workspace for anyone building daily habits, planning their time, and reflecting on progress. Study tools and spiritual practices are available, but your commitments are yours to choose.

Where are my records stored?

By default, your records stay in this browser on this device. They aren't encrypted by DAYSTAR or automatically synced. Export backups in Settings regularly: clearing browser data can erase your records. Existing optional cloud connection tools require your own configuration.

Is the coach powered by AI?

The current coach uses built-in coaching rules and your local records. It helps with check-ins and reviews; it does not connect to an external AI service.

Can I install it and use it offline?

Yes. DAYSTAR is a web app you can add to your home screen on supported browsers. Open the workspace online once to cache it. Core local tools then work offline; cloud connections need internet. Install options vary by browser, and reminders require the app to remain open.

Do I need an account?

No account is required for the local workspace. Open DAYSTAR, adjust the starter commitments, and begin. Installing it does not transfer records between browsers or devices.
